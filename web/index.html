Pick of the Week — Scored

Each week's single pick is frozen on the weekend and scored after Friday's close, Monday's open to Friday's close. A hit counts only if the stock moved at least 0.3% — smaller moves are "no call". Every result is shown next to a coin flip, the market, the sector fund, a random stock from the same sector and the candidates it beat, because a pick that can't beat those means nothing.

On the Radar — Report Card

Every weekend Graha freezes its strongest sector picks for the coming week (one lead stock each, only when the sky signal is strong and consistent — so some weeks have few or none). After Friday's close each pick is scored against what really happened, Monday's open to Friday's close, next to the sector fund and the overall market. Only finished weeks show results.
